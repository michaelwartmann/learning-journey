Resources 资源

Everything built along the way, free to use: no account, no subscription. These tools were made for one learner's weak spots, so treat them as examples. The real lesson is that you can build tools like these for your own.

Exam-style trainers

They run in the browser. Progress is saved only on your own device.

三级阅读Reading Trainer

Timed HSK 3 reading drills laid out like the paper: an option sheet, numbered items and answer bubbles. Matching and gap-fill sets, a pace target, plus sentence-ordering and pinyin-to-character tabs.

Reading Parts 1–2 · Writing Parts 1–2 · timed

三级语法Grammar Trainer

25 HSK 3 grammar points in four modules, each practised in the question types of the exam, with a quick run-through mode for review.

就 / 才 · 把 · 被 · 比 · 了 · complements and more

Open ›

三级小故事Short Stories

Original short stories and dialogues at HSK 3 level. Tap a word for its meaning, show or hide pinyin, listen with your device's voice, then answer a few questions.

Reading stamina · listening · vocabulary in context

Open ›

写汉字Handwriting Check

Write characters from memory with your finger or mouse and compare with the model and its stroke order. A quick way to find which characters you really can write.

For the paper exam's writing part

Open ›

练习室Practice Room

Six short sets, one for each part of the HSK 3 paper, including listening with the built-in Chinese voice. Hanzi first, help on tap.

Listening · reading · writing samples
