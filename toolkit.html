Method

How to press the buttons honestly

The grading rule

Say the answer out loud before you flip.

Again: didn't know it, the wrong character, or mixed it up.
Hard: right, but slow or a stroke missing.
Good: right after a moment.
Easy: instant, and not failed earlier today.

The trap in the data

A card that comes back a few minutes after Again feels easy because you just saw it. In the review data, Easy was pressed right after Again 867 times, and only 47 % of those cards were remembered at the next review. That habit caused most of the problem cards. Rule: after Again, the best you give that card the same day is Good.

Loops and leeches

Second Again on the same card in one session: read a note on what it gets confused with, say a sentence with it, bury it. A leech gets fixed (a new sentence, a breakdown of the character), not just repeated.

Method

Exam rules learned the hard way

Listening

Read the three options before the audio starts and listen for those exact words. An option that only shares a sound with what you heard (太小 / 太旧) is a likely trap.

Matching

Name what the sentence does first: 几位 → a number of people, 怎么才 → a reason, 不是 → answers a yes/no guess. A shared character is a trap. Check the leftover pairs before the last pick: errors come in chains.

Gap-fill

Before reading, write n / v / adj / mw next to every word in the bank. Then fill by slot: 这___ + noun → measure word; 非常___ → adjective; 在…的___下 → noun.

Guess marking

Keep a scrap of paper next to the test and note every number you guessed. Afterwards: sure and wrong is the most valuable group (a real misconception); guessed and right was luck and needs review.

Freeze protocol

One item at a time; whisper it; commit. When it blurs: pen down, three slow breaths, next item. After 60 seconds on one item: guess, mark it, move on. Never leave a blank.

Sentence order

Before the verb: time, place, with whom. After it: 了, the amount, the result. Subject + time + 想/要 + verb + object. No time phrase at the end.

Working with AI

Small tools, built for one problem each

The AI part of this journey is less magic than it sounds. It is mostly four habits:

1. Export your data and ask questions about it. Flashcard review logs show exactly which words loop and why. Mock results show which part of the paper loses points.

2. Transcribe the listening audio. A speech-to-text model turns a mock's audio into text, so after the test you can see the one word you missed, instead of “the whole thing was too fast”.

3. Ask for patterns, not answers. Ask the AI to report section scores and error patterns only, without the questions or answers. The paper stays usable for a rerun.

4. Build a tiny trainer for each weak spot. The Practice Room shows what these look like: a timed gap-fill with word types, a matching set that trains “what does this sentence do?”, a sentence builder.

A prompt for words that won't stick

Paste once as the instructions of a custom assistant, then send it one word at a time.

You are a strict, efficient HSK tutor. I will send one Chinese word.
Answer in exactly this structure, with no introduction:

1. Meaning and mechanics
Meaning: [English]
For each character: [character] ([pinyin]): [meaning].
Radical: [radical] ([its meaning]).
Logic: [one sentence on why these parts make this character].

2. In context
Three sentences using only HSK 1–3 words:
one simple, one with more context, one from daily
conversation. Hanzi, pinyin with tone marks, English.

3. Don't confuse with
Up to two look-alike or sound-alike words, one line each.
Free official material

Where to find real HSK papers

Nothing beats a real paper under real time. These are free to download from the institutions that publish them. They are linked here, not copied.

Confucius Institute, University of Manchester: past papers with listening audio for several levels, plus HSK grammar-point lists.

Chinese Testing International (chinesetest.cn): the official exam body; syllabus and sample papers.

Cleveland State University: HSK 3 papers with transcripts and answers: H31002, H31003, H31005.

HSK 3.0 (new format): the official structure and sample items (PDF, Chinese), via Konfuzius-Institut Heidelberg.

How to use a paper: print it, time it, do listening and reading back to back (that's where stamina breaks), mark your guesses, and only then look at the answers.
