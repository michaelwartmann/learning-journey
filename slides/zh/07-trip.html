这是我的路。2026年是火马年，2027年2月6号以后是火羊年。我自己属羊，是土羊。 Zhè shì wǒ de lù. Èr líng èr liù nián shì huǒ mǎ nián, èr líng èr qī nián èr yuè liù hào yǐhòu shì huǒ yáng nián. Wǒ zìjǐ shǔ yáng, shì tǔ yáng. This is my road. 2026 is the year of the fire horse; from 6 February 2027 it is the fire goat. I was born in a goat year myself: an earth goat. 路上先有两个考试。十一月二十一号，我在法国米卢斯考旧版三级。十二月十三号，我在卡尔斯鲁厄考新版三级，新版有口语。 Lù shang xiān yǒu liǎng ge kǎoshì. Shíyī yuè èrshíyī hào, wǒ zài Fǎguó Mǐlúsī kǎo jiùbǎn sān jí. Shí'èr yuè shísān hào, wǒ zài Kǎ'ěrsīlǔ'è kǎo xīnbǎn sān jí, xīnbǎn yǒu kǒuyǔ. First there are two exams. On 21 November I take the old HSK 3 in Mulhouse, France. On 13 December the new HSK 3 in Karlsruhe, which includes speaking. 一月中我飞到中国，可能是上海。二月六号是春节：马变成羊。我应该在哪个城市过春节？我还不知道。我很想看无人机表演。 Yī yuè zhōng wǒ fēi dào Zhōngguó, kěnéng shì Shànghǎi. Èr yuè liù hào shì Chūnjié: mǎ biànchéng yáng. Wǒ yīnggāi zài nǎge chéngshì guò Chūnjié? Wǒ hái bù zhīdào. Wǒ hěn xiǎng kàn wúrénjī biǎoyǎn. In mid-January I fly to China, maybe to Shanghai. 6 February is Spring Festival: the horse becomes a goat. Which city should I celebrate in? I don't know yet. I'd love to see a drone show. 然后我去旅行。这些图片是我想在中国看的东西：冰和雪、寺庙和山、风筝、熊猫、辣的菜、沙漠、新科技。你认识哪个地方吗？ Ránhòu wǒ qù lǚxíng. Zhèxiē túpiàn shì wǒ xiǎng zài Zhōngguó kàn de dōngxi: bīng hé xuě, sìmiào hé shān, fēngzheng, xióngmāo, là de cài, shāmò, xīn kējì. Nǐ rènshi nǎge dìfang ma? Then I travel. These pictures are things I want to see in China: ice and snow, temples and mountains, kites, pandas, spicy food, the desert, new technology. Do you recognise a place? 三月十四号，我想在中国考四级。三月底我回家。你觉得我应该在哪儿过春节？ Sān yuè shísì hào, wǒ xiǎng zài Zhōngguó kǎo sì jí. Sān yuè dǐ wǒ huí jiā. Nǐ juéde wǒ yīnggāi zài nǎr guò Chūnjié? On 14 March I want to take HSK 4 in China. At the end of March I go home. Where do you think I should spend Spring Festival?
