04 · 迈克尔的中文路线图

我为什么做这些？为了说话：跟中国人说话，在课上说话。说话是生活在新语言里最直接的方式。可是说话需要很多基础，我现在说得还很少。 Wǒ wèishénme zuò zhèxiē? Wèile shuōhuà: gēn Zhōngguó rén shuōhuà, zài kè shang shuōhuà. Shuōhuà shì shēnghuó zài xīn yǔyán lǐ zuì zhíjiē de fāngshì. Kěshì shuōhuà xūyào hěn duō jīchǔ, wǒ xiànzài shuō de hái hěn shǎo. Why am I doing all this? To speak: with Chinese people, and in my lessons. Speaking is the most direct way of living in a new language. But it needs a lot of foundation, and my speaking is still very limited. 第一个问题是语法，我有一点儿骄傲。上学的时候，语法对我来说一直很简单。中文没有动词变化，也没有格，所以我觉得中文语法很容易。可是中文语法很细：了、就和才、把、语序。错了，意思就变了。 Dì-yī ge wèntí shì yǔfǎ, wǒ yǒu yìdiǎnr jiāo'ào. Shàngxué de shíhou, yǔfǎ duì wǒ lái shuō yìzhí hěn jiǎndān. Zhōngwén méiyǒu dòngcí biànhuà, yě méiyǒu gé, suǒyǐ wǒ juéde Zhōngwén yǔfǎ hěn róngyì. Kěshì Zhōngwén yǔfǎ hěn xì: le, jiù hé cái, bǎ, yǔxù. Cuò le, yìsi jiù biàn le. The first problem is grammar, and I was a bit arrogant. At school grammar was always easy for me. Chinese has no verb endings and no cases, so I thought Chinese grammar was easy. But it is very subtle: 了, 就 and 才, 把, word order. Get it wrong and the meaning changes. 第二和第三个问题是阅读和听力。问题就是速度。汉字我认识，可是读得很慢；别人说得快，我就跟不上。 Dì-èr hé dì-sān ge wèntí shì yuèdú hé tīnglì. Wèntí jiù shì sùdù. Hànzì wǒ rènshi, kěshì dú de hěn màn; biérén shuō de kuài, wǒ jiù gēn bu shàng. The second and third problems are reading and listening. The problem is simply speed. I know the characters but read slowly; when people speak fast, I can't keep up. 第四个问题我完全没想到：手写。我以为可以打字。后来我才知道考试用纸和笔，每个汉字都要自己写。所以我从零开始。 Dì-sì ge wèntí wǒ wánquán méi xiǎngdào: shǒuxiě. Wǒ yǐwéi kěyǐ dǎzì. Hòulái wǒ cái zhīdào kǎoshì yòng zhǐ hé bǐ, měi ge Hànzì dōu yào zìjǐ xiě. Suǒyǐ wǒ cóng líng kāishǐ. The fourth problem I didn't see coming at all: handwriting. I thought I could type. Only later did I learn the exam is on paper, and every character must be written by hand. So I started from zero. 语法要理解。阅读和听力要多读、多听。手写要多练。 Yǔfǎ yào lǐjiě. Yuèdú hé tīnglì yào duō dú, duō tīng. Shǒuxiě yào duō liàn. Grammar needs understanding. Reading and listening need lots of reading and listening. Handwriting needs practice.
