06 · 迈克尔的中文路线图

这是一个金字塔，一切都在里面。 Zhè shì yí gè jīnzìtǎ, yíqiè dōu zài lǐmiàn. This is a pyramid; everything fits into it. 最下面是Duolingo和Anki。Duolingo给了我第一批词、魔法和对语言的感觉。Anki帮我打好最重要的词的基础。 Zuì xiàmiàn shì Duolingo hé Anki. Duolingo gěile wǒ dì-yī pī cí, mófǎ hé duì yǔyán de gǎnjué. Anki bāng wǒ dǎhǎo zuì zhòngyào de cí de jīchǔ. At the bottom are Duolingo and Anki. Duolingo gave me my first words, the magic and a feel for the language. Anki builds a solid base of the most important words. 上面是阅读和听力。阅读没有时间压力，可是汉字特别难。听力有时间压力，因为要跟上说话的速度，可是它更像拼音，比读汉字容易。 Shàngmiàn shì yuèdú hé tīnglì. Yuèdú méiyǒu shíjiān yālì, kěshì Hànzì tèbié nán. Tīnglì yǒu shíjiān yālì, yīnwèi yào gēn shàng shuōhuà de sùdù, kěshì tā gèng xiàng pīnyīn, bǐ dú Hànzì róngyì. Above that are reading and listening. Reading has no time pressure, but Hanzi is extra hard. Listening has time pressure, because you must keep up with the speed of speech, but it is closer to pinyin, so it is easier than reading Hanzi. 再上面是打字：我打拼音，手机或者电脑写汉字。 Zài shàngmiàn shì dǎzì: wǒ dǎ pīnyīn, shǒujī huòzhě diànnǎo xiě Hànzì. Above that is typing: I type pinyin, and the phone or computer writes the Hanzi. 最上面是说话。说话的时候，魔法就来了。快乐来自我的老师：有人回答我。 Zuì shàngmiàn shì shuōhuà. Shuōhuà de shíhou, mófǎ jiù lái le. Kuàilè láizì wǒ de lǎoshī: yǒu rén huídá wǒ. At the very top is speaking. When I speak, the magic happens. The happiness comes from my teacher: someone who answers me.
