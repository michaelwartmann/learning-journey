三级的词我都会了，那就没问题了，对吧？ Sān jí de cí wǒ dōu huì le, nà jiù méi wèntí le, duì ba? If I know all the HSK 3 words, I'll be fine, right? 所以我做了工程师会做的事。我下载了一套网上的Anki卡片，用AI调好了设置。然后我让AI把它做成很现代的卡片，我拼命地学，想最快学会新版三级的一千个词。我还做了写字卡片：看拼音，写汉字，再跟标准字比较。 Suǒyǐ wǒ zuòle gōngchéngshī huì zuò de shì. Wǒ xiàzǎile yí tào wǎngshang de Anki kǎpiàn, yòng AI tiáohǎole shèzhì. Ránhòu wǒ ràng AI bǎ tā zuòchéng hěn xiàndài de kǎpiàn, wǒ pīnmìng de xué, xiǎng zuì kuài xuéhuì xīnbǎn sān jí de yìqiān ge cí. Wǒ hái zuòle xiězì kǎpiàn: kàn pīnyīn, xiě Hànzì, zài gēn biāozhǔn zì bǐjiào. So I did what an engineer does. I downloaded an Anki deck and tuned the settings with AI. Then I had AI turn it into a very modern deck, and I went all in to learn the 1,000 words of the new HSK 3 as fast as possible. I also made a handwriting deck: see the pinyin, write the character, compare it with the model. 现在没问题了吧？不！每个词我都认识，可是这没有用。我读得慢，听得慢，语法也不太懂。 Xiànzài méi wèntí le ba? Bù! Měi ge cí wǒ dōu rènshi, kěshì zhè méiyǒu yòng. Wǒ dú de màn, tīng de màn, yǔfǎ yě bú tài dǒng. So now I'm fine, right? No! I know every word, but that's no use. I read slowly, listen slowly, and don't really understand the grammar. 只学词是不行的。词活在句子里：学语言要靠语境，也要去感觉这门语言。 Zhǐ xué cí shì bù xíng de. Cí huó zài jùzi lǐ: xué yǔyán yào kào yǔjìng, yě yào qù gǎnjué zhè mén yǔyán. Learning only words doesn't work. Words live in sentences: learning a language depends on context, and on feeling the language.
