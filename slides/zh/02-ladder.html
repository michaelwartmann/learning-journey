02 · 迈克尔的中文路线图

现在说说我学中文的路，我现在在哪儿。 Xiànzài shuōshuo wǒ xué Zhōngwén de lù, wǒ xiànzài zài nǎr. Now about my Chinese learning journey, and where I stand. 去年我开始用Duolingo，我很喜欢中文。我很尊重汉字，因为我的字一直写得不好看，德语也是。可是后来我喜欢上了汉字，还给女儿画了一张生日画。 Qùnián wǒ kāishǐ yòng Duolingo, wǒ hěn xǐhuan Zhōngwén. Yì kāishǐ wǒ yǒudiǎnr pà dú Hànzì, xiě Hànzì, kěshì hòulái wǒ xǐhuan shàng le, hái gěi nǚ'ér huàle yì zhāng shēngrì huà. Last year I started with Duolingo and liked Chinese a lot. I had a lot of respect for Hanzi, because my handwriting was never good, not even in German. But then I came to love it, and I even drew a birthday picture for my daughter. 今年我重新开始了：用Du Chinese阅读，用一点儿Duolingo，还用Anki卡片。后来我开始上网课，非常有意思。 Jīnnián wǒ chóngxīn kāishǐ le: yòng Du Chinese yuèdú, yòng yìdiǎnr Duolingo, hái yòng Anki kǎpiàn. Hòulái wǒ kāishǐ shàng wǎngkè, fēicháng yǒu yìsi. This year I started again: reading with Du Chinese, a little Duolingo, and Anki flashcards. Then I started online lessons, which is really fun. 我没上过正式的中文课，所以我常常说错，简单的话也说不好。可是我开始用HSK考试测试自己：学了一个半月，一级模拟考试我考了满分。没想到二级也过了。三级一开始太难了，因为都是汉字，第一次模拟考试我刚刚及格。可是现在我每天做一次模拟考试，分数越来越高：181、210，最近一次241分（满分300）。 Wǒ méi shàngguo zhèngshì de Zhōngwén kè, suǒyǐ wǒ chángcháng shuō cuò, jiǎndān de huà yě shuō bu hǎo. Kěshì wǒ kāishǐ yòng HSK kǎoshì cèshì zìjǐ: xuéle yí ge bàn yuè, yī jí mónǐ kǎoshì wǒ kǎole mǎnfēn. Méi xiǎngdào èr jí yě guò le. Sān jí yì kāishǐ tài nán le, yīnwèi dōu shì Hànzì, dì yī cì mónǐ kǎoshì wǒ gānggāng jígé. Kěshì xiànzài wǒ měitiān zuò yí cì mónǐ kǎoshì, fēnshù yuè lái yuè gāo: yìbǎi bāshíyī, èrbǎi yīshí, zuìjìn yí cì èrbǎi sìshíyī fēn (mǎnfēn sānbǎi). I never took a proper Chinese class, so I make many mistakes and can't say simple things well. But I started testing myself with the HSK: after a month and a half I got full marks in the level 1 practice test. To my surprise I passed level 2 too. Level 3 was very hard at first, because it is all Hanzi: I only just passed my first practice test. But now I do a practice test every day and my scores keep rising: 181, 210, and most recently 241 out of 300. 现在我在准备三级考试，十一月二十一号考。十二月我再考一次，因为新的三级更难。2027年我想考四级。 Xiànzài wǒ zài zhǔnbèi sān jí kǎoshì, shíyī yuè èrshíyī hào kǎo. Shí'èr yuè wǒ zài kǎo yí cì, yīnwèi xīn de sān jí gèng nán. Èr líng èr qī nián wǒ xiǎng kǎo sì jí. Now I'm preparing for HSK 3; the exam is on 21 November. In December I take it again, because the new HSK 3 is harder. In 2027 I want to take HSK 4.
