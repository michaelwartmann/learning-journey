A core grid of bathhouse cities

Core: nine cities with a 24-hour bathhouse: Shanghai, Hangzhou, Chengdu, Chongqing, Shenzhen, Guangzhou, Fuzhou, Beijing, Yangzhou.

Worth it anyway: Guilin for the karst and the Li River by bike, Xi'an for the Terracotta Army, Zigong for the lanterns.

Rhythm: two nights on the train, one in a bathhouse, a hostel when needed.

A2 · Appendix: train nights, bathhouse days

The old bathhouse tradition is strongest in the north and around Shanghai. The new 24-hour spas, with saunas, buffets and sleeping halls, are now in every big city, in the south too. So the route is a grid of bathhouse cities. A place without one is only on the list if there is something special to see or do.
