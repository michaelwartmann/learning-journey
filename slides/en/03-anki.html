03 · My Mandarin Roadmap

If I know all the words for HSK 3, I should be fine, right? So I did what an engineer does. I downloaded a public Anki deck and fine-tuned the settings with AI. Then I asked AI to turn it into a very modern learning deck, and I went all in to learn all 1,000 words of the new HSK 3 as fast as I could. I even built a handwriting deck: you see the pinyin over a gap, draw the character, and compare it with the model. Now I should be fine, right? No! I know all the words, but that means nothing. I am slow at reading and listening, and I don’t know the grammar properly. Learning the words alone does not work. Words live in sentences: the learning comes from context, and from feeling the language.
