南方的澡堂够不够？

Nánfāng de zǎotáng gòu bu gòu?

Are there enough bathhouses in the south?

A1 · Appendix to My Mandarin Roadmap

The idea: sleep on night trains, and between trains spend a day in a 24-hour bathhouse. A bathhouse has a sauna, showers, fruit and food, and a quiet hall to sleep in. Bathhouses are often called a northern thing. So: are there enough of them in the south? The next slides check, city by city.
