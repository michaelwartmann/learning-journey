StopPickWhy
9 Fuzhou 福州Old bathhouses at Hot Spring Park, e.g. FuquanA city on natural hot springs, used since the Tang dynasty; the water comes out at about 40 °C.
10 Beijing 北京Shuiguo 水裹汤泉, Sihui
or 雅致汤泉, Daxing (budget)		A favourite with foreigners at about €43, with unlimited fruit, drinks and ice cream; Yazhi is about €13 for 18 hours.
11 Xi'an 西安Huaqing Palace hot springsWorth it for the Terracotta Army and the old imperial hot springs. For a night: search 汤泉 on Meituan.
12 Yangzhou 扬州Yongningquan bathhouseBirthplace of southern bathing: old tiled pools and 敲背 qiāo bèi, the drum-on-the-back massage.

One or two picks per city: a 24-hour place with a sauna, showers, fruit or food, and somewhere to sleep. Prices are in euro and only a guide; I check them on Meituan or Dianping before going, and I always bring my passport.
