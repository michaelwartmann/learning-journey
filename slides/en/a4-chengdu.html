Spring Festival in Chengdu · 成都过年

Temple fair 武侯祠大庙会

Lanterns, Sichuan opera and snack streets at Wuhou Shrine. In 2026 it ran 13 Feb–3 Mar; booked on WeChat with a passport.

Panda Lantern Festival 熊猫灯会

China's longest-running city lantern festival, since 1962. Book early: most days sold out in 2026.

Day trip: Zigong lanterns 自贡灯会

Called the best lanterns in China, 1–1.5 h by fast train. Peak ticket ≈ €22. Closed on New Year's Eve.

Bike days 骑行

A 100 km car-free ring with no traffic lights; sport bike ≈ €8–16 a day. Or two days out to Dujiangyan and Mount Qingcheng.

A4 · Appendix: train nights, bathhouse days · 2026 editions; 2027 follows New Year on 6 Feb

Chengdu is the place for Spring Festival: a big temple fair, lantern festivals, and in Zigong, about an hour away by fast train, the most famous lanterns in China. Chengdu is also flat and has a 100 km ring of bike paths without cars or traffic lights. And Mount Qingcheng, a Taoist holy mountain, is a two-day ride away. A bathhouse in Chengdu costs about €13 for 20 hours.
