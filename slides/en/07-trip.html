07 · My Mandarin Roadmap

This is my road. 2026 is the year of the fire horse, and on 6 February 2027 it becomes the year of the fire goat. I was born in a goat year myself: I am an earth goat. The road starts with two exams. On 21 November I take the old HSK 3 in Mulhouse, in France. On 13 December I take the new HSK 3 in Karlsruhe, and the new one includes speaking. On 21 January I fly from Frankfurt to Shanghai: my ticket is booked! Then comes Spring Festival on 6 February: the horse becomes a goat. In which city should I celebrate? I don’t know yet. I would love to see a drone show in the sky. After that I travel. These pictures are ideas of what I would love to see in China: snow and ice, temples and mountains, kites, pandas, spicy food, the desert, new technology. Do you recognise a place? On 14 March I want to take HSK 4 in Shanghai. On 18 March I fly home. Where do you think I should spend Spring Festival?
