05 · My Mandarin Roadmap

So, what does an engineer do with four gaps? He builds a tool for every gap. With AI, each one took only an evening or two. For reading, a trainer that looks exactly like the exam, with a timer. In October one matching item took me four minutes; a few weeks later, less than one. For listening and reading speed, short stories: I read along, listen, and answer questions. For grammar, a grammar trainer with 25 grammar points and a quick run-through every day. For writing, exercises in the exam format: put the words in the right order, and write the character for the pinyin. And for handwriting, a trainer and a flashcard deck where I draw every character and compare it with the model. You can open every tool on this website and try it yourself. So now there is a tool for everything… except speaking. And speaking is what it is all for. Speaking is interactive. If nobody answers, I am only reading aloud into the ether, and nothing comes back to me. Again, it looks like technology solves every problem, just like with the words. It helps a lot. But it is not enough.
