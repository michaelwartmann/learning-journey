04 · My Mandarin Roadmap

Why am I doing all this? To speak: with people in China, and in my lessons. Speaking is the most direct, active way of living in a new language. But speaking stands on everything else, and my speaking is still very limited. I know the words, but I can’t use them well yet. There are four gaps. The first gap is grammar, and here I was a little arrogant. At school, grammar was always easy for me; I have a mathematical mind. Chinese has no verb endings and no cases, so I thought: Chinese grammar is easy! But Chinese grammar is very subtle: 了, 就 and 才, 把, word order. If I get it wrong, the meaning changes, and I can’t say or understand the subtle things. The second and third gaps are reading and listening. Here the problem is simply speed. I know the characters, but I read slowly, and when people speak at normal speed I fall behind. The fourth gap was not even on my radar: handwriting. I just assumed I could type. Then I realised the exam is on paper, and I have to write every character by hand. So I started from zero. Grammar needs understanding. Reading and listening need exposure: a lot of reading and a lot of listening. And handwriting needs practice.
