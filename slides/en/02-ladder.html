02 · My Mandarin Roadmap

Now about my Chinese learning, and where I stand. Last year I started with Duolingo and liked Chinese a lot. I had a lot of respect for Hanzi, because my handwriting was never good, not even in my own language. But then I started to enjoy it, and I even made some art for my daughter Mallika’s 12th birthday. This year I started again: reading with Du Chinese, a little Duolingo, and Anki flashcards. Then I began online lessons on language-learning platforms, which is really fun. I never took a proper class in Chinese, so I make many mistakes and can’t say simple things yet. But I started testing myself with the HSK system. After one and a half months, I passed HSK 1 perfectly in a practice test. Then, to my surprise, I also passed HSK 2. HSK 3 was very hard at first, because it is all Hanzi: I only just passed my first practice test. But now I do a practice test every day, and my scores keep going up: 181, then 210, and most recently 241 out of 300. Now I am preparing for HSK 3, and I take the exam on 21 November. In December I take it again, because a new, harder version of HSK 3 is being introduced. In 2027 I want to take HSK 4.
