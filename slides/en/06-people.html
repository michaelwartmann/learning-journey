06 · My Mandarin Roadmap

Here is how it all fits together: a pyramid. At the bottom are Duolingo and Anki. Duolingo gave me my first words, the magic, and a feel for the language. Anki builds a solid base of the most important words. On top of that come reading and listening. Reading is great because there is normally no time pressure, but in Hanzi it is extra hard. Listening has time pressure, because you have to decode at the speed people speak, but it is closer to pinyin, so it is easier than reading Hanzi. On top of reading and listening comes typing: I type pinyin, and the phone or computer turns it into Hanzi. And at the very top is speaking. Speaking is where the magic happens. And the happiness comes from my teacher: someone who answers.
