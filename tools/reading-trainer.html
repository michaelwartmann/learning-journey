Writing section of the exam, built from the words one learner kept missing: part 1 puts words in order, part 2 gives pinyin and you write the character by hand.

每天30分钟 · Daily 30 minutes until 21 Nov

  1. One set here (5 min). Function first: for each question, name what it needs before you look for the answer: an offer needs yes/no thanks, 怎么/为什么 needs a reason, 什么时候/几点 needs a time, a 吗 question needs yes or no. A word that appears in both the question and an option is often a trap (sets 七 and 十 are built on it). Read all six options first and underline one key word in each. Answer the sure ones first. Used options grey out automatically: that's the elimination. Leave the last one for last, never force it: before the last pick, check that both remaining pairs make sense. One wrong pick almost always costs two points.
  2. A shared word proves nothing. It's a reason to look twice, not to rule an option in or out. 怎么 has two jobs: with 这么 / 才 / 没 / 又 it asks why (answer: a reason or an apology); with a plain verb it asks how (answer: a way or a method). Sets 13 and 14 train exactly this.
  3. One real exam block (10 min). Reading Part 1 or Part 2 (10 items) from an official past paper, on paper, with a timer. Target: 10 minutes, then 8.
  4. Repeated reading (10 min). Pick one short text (a Du Chinese story at HSK 3 level, or a Part 3 passage). Read it three times, timing each read. Reading the same text again until it flows is one of the few reading methods with direct evidence for Chinese: it raised fluency, character recognition and comprehension, and the gains carried over to new texts.
  5. Misses to Anki (5 min). Every word that slowed you down gets a Hanzi-only sentence card.
