Appendix: train nights, bathhouse days 澡堂地图

A rough plan for the China trip: sleep on night trains, and between trains spend a day in a 24-hour bathhouse with a sauna, showers, fruit and a quiet hall to sleep in. A map, the dates, Spring Festival in Chengdu and one or two bathhouses per city. Point at (or tap) any Chinese character to see its pinyin.

A personal, non-commercial learning journal. Prices and opening hours are only a guide and change often: check before you go. Map data: Natural Earth.
