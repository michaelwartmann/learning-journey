Appendix: train nights, bathhouse days 澡堂地图

A rough plan for the China trip: sleep on night trains, and between trains spend a day in a 24-hour bathhouse with a sauna, showers, fruit and a quiet hall to sleep in. A map, the dates, Spring Festival in Chengdu and one or two bathhouses per city. Point at (or tap) any Chinese character to see its pinyin.

Practise the situations · 先说中文

Every stop on this route is also a conversation. The rule for the trip: try it in Chinese first, and switch to English only when nothing else works. The speaking trainer starts with the HSK 3 speaking test; these situations become its next decks.

  • 买卧铺票 Buying a sleeper ticket and finding the berth
  • 澡堂过夜 Bathhouse: ticket, locker band, staying the night
  • 青旅入住 Hostel check-in with a passport
  • 租自行车 Renting a bike for the greenway
  • 点一碗面 Ordering noodles and street food
  • 问路 Asking the way and taking the metro
  • 过年好 Spring Festival greetings and the temple fair
  • 在火车上聊天 Small talk on the night train
