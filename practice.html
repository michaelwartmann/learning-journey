Small, original HSK 3–level sets, built the same way as the real paper

The talk Resources

These are not mock exams. They are short samples showing what kind of small tool any learner can build, with AI help, around their own weak spots. Each set copies the format of one part of the HSK 3 paper (old format: 40 listening, 30 reading, 10 writing items). Every sentence here was written for this site using HSK 1–3 vocabulary.

Hanzi first. Tap Help on an item to see pinyin and English, and Check when you're done. For full official papers, see the free links in the Toolkit.

All practice items are original and free to reuse for non-commercial learning. Listening uses your device's built-in Chinese voice.
